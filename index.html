Bio

Founded in 1996 by Delarvoth, Actum Est is the manifestation of a singular and uncompromising vision: a project forged in solitude and shaped by introspection.

Its music builds immersive soundscapes where darkness becomes language. Each composition unfolds like a ritual, where cold melodies and oppressive harmonies converge into a vast, introspective experience.

Every composition, arrangement and instrument is written and performed by Delarvoth alone, rooted in the raw tradition of the ’90s and shaped by dedicated studio work. Now released on digital platforms, the work keeps its obscure essence while reaching beyond the underground.

Visual identity developed with the support of Christophe Szpajdel and the artistic collaboration of Stanislav Krawczyk.

What is done cannot be undone. Actum Est
